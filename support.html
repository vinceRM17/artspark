Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a new prompt?

ArtSpark gives you a fresh art prompt each day on the Home screen. You can change how often prompts arrive (daily, weekdays, weekly, and more) under Settings → Prompt Frequency.

How do I change my daily reminder time?

Go to Settings → Notifications, turn on Daily Reminders, and tap Reminder Time to pick when your prompt arrives. If reminders don't appear, make sure notifications are enabled for ArtSpark in your device's Settings.

How do I set my art supplies, subjects, and skill level?

Open Settings → My Supplies & Preferences to tell ArtSpark which mediums you have on hand, which subjects you enjoy, and anything you'd rather skip. Set your skill level under Settings → Skill Level so prompts match where you are.

How do I reset my prompt history?

Under Settings → Danger Zone, tap Reset Prompt History. This clears your saved prompts, responses, and artwork. It cannot be undone.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Danger Zone and tap Delete Account. After you confirm, your account and all associated data (prompts, responses, artwork, streaks, and preferences) are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

Is ArtSpark free?

Yes. ArtSpark is free to download and use.
